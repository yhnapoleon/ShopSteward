PROJECT EVIDENCE & READING NOTES
  • This is a logical module map. Business, Agent and Knowledge workers are distinct processes. Business, simulation and Knowledge state use separate databases.
  • The Agent calls business HTTP tools. Forecast and document requests are routed through backend integrations. ML and model output do not directly change the business ledger.
  • The task-evaluation harness is experimental and separate from the general Agent loop. Data/SFT, self-hosted task models, multi-agent orchestration and RL are proposed.
  • Qwen3-8B, LoRA and vLLM are candidate choices. GRPO/PPO are possible RL algorithms, subject to evaluation. Real-store connectors and cross-product planning are longer-term extensions.
