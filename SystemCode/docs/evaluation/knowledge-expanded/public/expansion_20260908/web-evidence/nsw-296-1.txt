L0@P0: Food Authority
L1@P0: Anyone can donate food in NSW; however, the food must be safe to eat.
L2@P0: Law on donating food
L3@P0: Before you donate food to a charitable or not-for-profit organisation you should contact them to find
L4@P0: out what they can or cannot accept. Follow food safety standards to ensure the food provided is safe.
L5@P0: The Civil Liability Act 2002 limits the liability of individuals and businesses that donate food, providing
L6@P0: certain food safety conditions have been met:
L7@P0: • Food is donated in good faith for a charitable or benevolent purpose.
L8@P0: • Food is donated with the intention that the receiver of the food does not have to pay for the food.
L9@P0: • Food is safe to eat when it leaves the possession or control of the donor.
L10@P0: • The donor gives the recipient any information it needs to ensure the ongoing safety of the food,
L11@P0: regarding both food handling and time limits for safe consumption.
L12@P0: ‘Use by’ and ‘best before’ dates
L13@P0: Food should not be donated or eaten after its ‘use by’ date because it may be unsafe to eat, even
L14@P0: though spoilage may not be visible.
L15@P0: Charities that receive food that will pass its use by date before distribution should throw the food away.
L16@P0: Food marked as ‘best before’ can be given away after the best before date has passed, provided the
L17@P0: food is not damaged, deteriorated or perished.
L18@P0: There may be some loss of quality in food after its best before date but as long as it is otherwise fit for
L19@P0: human consumption, it is not illegal to sell or distribute this food, nor should there be any safety risk
L20@P0: from eating the food.
L21@P0: Keeping donated food safe
L22@P0: Whether you are a business donating food to a charity or a charity distributing food to individuals,
L23@P0: always follow standard food safety when transporting food:
L24@P0: • Check the food for spoilage to ensure it is safe and fit for human consumption.
L25@P0: • Check the date marking on food packaging and throw away any food that is past its use by date.
L26@P0: • Observe temperature control requirements of potentially hazardous food. If the food must be kept
L27@P0: below 5°C or above 60°C, tell the recipient of the required temperature.
L28@P0: • If the food will only be safe to eat for a limited time, tell the recipient of that time period.
L29@P0: • Cook food thoroughly.
