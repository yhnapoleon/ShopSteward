L0@P0: Wet storage refers to the re-immersion of shellfish to
L1@P0: store, remove sand or improve condition.
L2@P0: The wet storage of shellfish poses unique and significant
L3@P0: food safety risks. Due to their mode of feeding by
L4@P0: indiscriminate filtration, bivalve shellfish are by nature a
L5@P0: high-risk food. They feed by drawing water across the gill
L6@P0: surface, which then traps particles from the water and
L7@P0: transports them to the mouth for ingestion. As they feed,
L8@P0: bacteria, viruses and harmful substances that are in the
L9@P0: water will be accumulated in the shellfish. Water used for
L10@P0: wet storage must be sourced from a clean area and
L11@P0: maintained in a manner that prevents the build-up of
L12@P0: pathogens and other contaminants. Proper construction,
L13@P0: operation and maintenance of wet storage systems is
L14@P0: crucial to the safe wet storage of shellfish.
L15@P0: Shellfish Holding System Design
L16@P0: • All equipment in contact with water must be durable,
L17@P0: non-toxic, non-corrosive and easily cleanable.
L18@P0: • The system must include a UV disinfection unit (or
L19@P0: equivalent alternative water disinfection system
L20@P0: approved for use by NSW Food Authority) capable of
L21@P0: maintaining a zero faecal coliform count at the entry
L22@P0: point to the tank (e.g. at the spray bar). The
L23@P0: disinfection unit must be capable of achieving a
L24@P0: 99.9% (3 log) kill rate for E.coli at the flow rate
L25@P0: generated by the water circulation pumps.
L26@P0: • Shellfish holding systems must have completely
L27@P0: separate water circulation systems from other
L28@P0: species (such as finfish or crustaceans). Do NOT
L29@P0: place shellfish in the same tank as finfish or
L30@P0: crustaceans.
L31@P0: • Shellfish holding systems should exhibit good water
L32@P0: flow with no dead spots.
L33@P0: • A cooling system should be included in the design;
L34@P0: water temperature for shellfish wet storage systems
L35@P0: should be maintained below 15°C (<10°C is
L36@P0: recommended for most species).
L37@P0: • Tank or spray systems are both acceptable.
L38@P0: Shellfish Holding System Operation and
L39@P0: Maintenance
L40@P0: • The entire system should be cleaned and sanitised
L41@P0: prior to each use.
L42@P0: • Source water must be free of any contaminants.
L43@P0: Ocean water must be sourced from a clean area
L44@P0: away from outfalls or shorefront development such
L45@P0: as marinas. Each draw of water for wet storage
L46@P0: purposes must be batch tested with a result of 14
L47@P0: faecal coliforms/100ml or less. Ocean water
L48@P0: suppliers should provide a laboratory test report for
L49@P0: each batch and a declaration that the water has
L50@P0: been sourced from a clean area.
L51@P0: • Shellfish should be culled to remove dead or
L52@P0: damaged animals and free of mud/debris prior to
L53@P0: being placed into wet storage.
L54@P0: • Shellfish should be inspected, and dead shellfish
L55@P0: removed at least daily.
L56@P0: • The disinfection system must be on while shellfish
L57@P0: are being wet stored.
L58@P0: • Shellfish from different lots/batches should be kept
L59@P0: separate to maintain product traceability.
L60@P0: • Temperature records should be maintained (AM &
L61@P0: PM each day is recommended).
