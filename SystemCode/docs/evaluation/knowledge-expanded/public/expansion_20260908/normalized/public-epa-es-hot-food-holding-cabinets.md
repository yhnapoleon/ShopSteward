## Why ENERGY STAR?

[Origin: https://www.energystar.gov/products/commercial_hot_food_holding_cabinets]

ENERGY STAR certified hot food holding cabinets often incorporate better insulation which reduces heat loss, offer better temperature uniformity within the cabinet from top to bottom, and keeps the external cabinet cooler. In addition, many certified holding cabinets may include energy saving devices such as magnetic door gaskets, auto-door closures, or Dutch doors.

### Savings figures:

[Origin: https://www.energystar.gov/products/commercial_hot_food_holding_cabinets]

- 40 percent more energy efficient than standard models

- Save businesses $70 annually and on average $590 on utility bills over the product’s lifetime when compared to standard models

- Save businesses 540 kWh annually

### Eligible products:

[Origin: https://www.energystar.gov/products/commercial_hot_food_holding_cabinets]

- Glass or solid door cabinets (fully enclosed compartment with one or more doors)

### Ineligible products:

[Origin: https://www.energystar.gov/products/commercial_hot_food_holding_cabinets]

- Dual function equipment (e.g., cook-and-hold and proofing units)

- Heated transparent merchandising cabinets

- Drawer warmers.
