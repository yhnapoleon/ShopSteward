Commercial Food Service Equipment

ENERGY STAR certified commercial kitchen equipment helps cafe, restaurant, and institutional kitchens save energy by cutting utility and maintenance costs without sacrificing features, quality, or style. Saving energy helps save money on utility bills and protect the environment by reducing greenhouse gas emissions.

Outfitting a commercial kitchen with a suite of commercial food service equipment that has earned the ENERGY STAR could save operators about 350 MMBTU/year, or about $4,000/year.  Design an Energy Efficient Restaurant or Commercial Kitchen (PDF, 5.4 MB).

FAQs

Can commercial food service dealers and distributors join the ENERGY STAR program?

Yes, commercial food service distributors can join the ENERGY STAR program as product retailers. Please visit the Retailers Resources page for information on joining, obtaining training, and promoting ENERGY STAR.

Who do I contact if I spot a logo violation?

Visit the ENERGY STAR Brand Guidelines webpage for instructions on verifying the potential logo violation and send an email to our online help desk. The ENERGY STAR enforcement team will follow their process to address the violation.
