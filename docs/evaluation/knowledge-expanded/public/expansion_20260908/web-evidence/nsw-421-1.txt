L38@P2: Introduction
L39@P2: The NSW Food Authority is the government organisation that helps ensure food is safe and correctly
L40@P2: labelled. It works with consumers, industry, and other government organisations to minimise food
L41@P2: poisoning by providing information about and regulating the safe production, storage, transport,
L42@P2: promotion and preparation of food.
L43@P2: Food Authority food safety officers are authorised officers under the NSW Food Act 2003 (the Act)
L44@P2: and have wide powers, which include the power to enter and inspect premises and inspect any
L45@P2: records relating to the sale of food.
L46@P2: This Standard has been developed by the Food Authority to assist retail meat premises in meeting
L47@P2: the food safety and labelling requirements outlined in the Australia New Zealand Food Standards
L48@P2: Code (the Code), which is law in Australia.
L49@P2: While the Food Authority aims to inform industry and assist businesses with meeting their
L50@P2: responsibilities, under the Act the Food Authority can take enforcement action against any
L51@P2: businesses that fail to comply.
L52@P2: This Standard may not list all the legal requirements that your business needs to comply with.
L53@P2: Businesses should refer to the Act, Food Regulation 2025, and the Code for details on food safety
L54@P2: requirements.
L55@P2: NSW legislation can be found at legislation.nsw.gov.au and the Food Standards Code at
L56@P2-3: www.foodstandards.gov.au. NSW standard for construction and hygienic operations of retail meat premises

L58@P3: Purpose and scope
L59@P3: The Food Regulation 2025 (the Regulation) defines meat retail premises as:
L60@P3: • premises where meat (including poultry) is sold by retail and on which raw meat carcases or
L61@P3: parts of raw meat carcases are processed in some way, including by boning, slicing, or cutting.
L62@P3: ‘Butcher shop’ is the term commonly used for these premises and may include supermarket
L63@P3: operations and independent meat and poultry retail premises.
L64@P3: A retail meat premises does not include premises where:
L65@P3: a. in any week during the preceding calendar year, more than one tonne of meat was sold by
L66@P3: wholesale (these premises must be licensed as a meat processor), or
L67@P3: b. all meat is sold in a form ready to be consumed (such as is sold at a restaurant or take-away food
L68@P3: shop), or
L69@P3: c. all meat is sold in a form commonly referred to as cook and chill (that is, cooked packaged meat
L70@P3: that requires reheating before consumption).
L71@P3: The NSW Standard for Construction and Hygienic Operations of Retail Meat Premises (the
L72@P3: Standard) contains the minimum construction requirements and hygienic operation standards for
L73@P3: retail meat premises and forms part of the minimum standards for retail meat premises, as required
L74@P3: by the Regulation.
L75@P3: As part of the Standard, all retail meat premises must operate under an approved food safety
L76@P3: program based on Hazard Analysis Critical Control Point (HACCP) principles.
L77@P3: In addition, the requirements of Standard 3.2.1 – Food Safety Programs, Standard 3.2.2 – Food
L78@P3: Safety Practices and General Requirements and Standard 3.2.3 – Food Premises and Equipment of
L79@P3: the Food Standards Code have been considered in the Standard. For ease of interpretation, there
L80@P3: are sections which have been expanded to accommodate the specific requirements for retail meat
L81@P3: premises.
L82@P3: Alternative equivalent techniques or procedures to those detailed in the Standard may be used by
L83@P3: operators of retail meat premises, provided compliance with the overall goal can be verified using a
L84@P3-4: HACCP-based food safety program.NSW standard for construction and hygienic operations of retail meat premises

L91@P4: consumption.

L96@P4: Audit A programmed and clearly defined system of verifying that a quality
L97@P4: system is operating to standards defined in the Food Safety Program.
L98@P4: Clean In relation to:
L99@P4: a. carcases or meat - means free from all visible contaminants (such as
L100@P4: ingesta, dust, rail grease, wool, hair, faecal material, bile, excretions
L101@P4: and pathological conditions)
L102@P4: b. equipment and utensils - means free of visible contamination,
L103@P4: washed, sanitised and free of objectionable odours
L104@P4: c. premises - means surfaces of floors, walls, ceilings free of visible
L105@P4: contamination, washed, and free of objectionable odours
L106@P4: d. clothing, means free of visible contamination.
L107@P4: Construction The design, construction, equipment and maintenance of retail meat
L108@P4: premises.

L110@P4: microorganisms, that makes meat and meat products unsafe or
L111@P4: unsuitable for human consumption.

L113@P4: effective cleaning.
L114@P4: Cured A product is cured if curing salts have been added at a level which
L115@P4: preserves the product, being a minimum 2.5% salt on water phase and
L116@P4: 100 ppm nitrite in-going.
L117@P4: Edible Safe and suitable for human consumption.
