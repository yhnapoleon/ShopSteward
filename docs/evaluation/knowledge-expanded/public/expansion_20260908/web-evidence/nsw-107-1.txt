L577: Preparing for your regular council inspection will help your business get the best possible result.
L578: ## General
L579:   * Make sure your premises has an up-to-date notification record at your local council.
L580:   * Look at your last hygiene and food safety inspection report to check you’ve taken all the actions needed to meet the legal requirements. If you can’t find your last report, contact your local council and they will be able to give you a copy.
L581:   * Promptly arrange for any repairs and conduct regular maintenance of the premises and equipment.
L582:   * Perform a self check by going through the cite267†Food Premises Assessment Report (FPAR) (PDF, 314 KB), which local councils use to assess compliance.
L583: ## Food Safety Supervisor
L584: 
L585: If you need a Food Safety Supervisor, ensure their certificate is current and on the premises.

L628: ## Temperature control
L629: 
L630: Keep high risk food at or below 5°C or at or above 60°C during receival, processing, storage and display.
L631: 
L632: Use a thermometer to check food is at a safe temperature and cooked properly.
