L1@P0: Food Authority
L2@P0: Whole fruit and vegetables are safe and nutritious. However, when cut there is a
L3@P0: risk of contamination from harmful bacteria unless handled correctly.
L4@P0: Fresh fruit and vegetables eaten raw, without any cooking or heating to destroy bacteria, need safe
L5@P0: handling to minimise contamination and prevent harmful bacteria growing.
L6@P0: These tips are relevant for many types of cut fruit and vegetables eaten raw, such as fruit platters, fruit
L7@P0: salad, fruit kebabs, carrot and celery sticks, vegetable platters and salads.
L8@P0: Preparing
L9@P0: • Keep fruit and vegetables separate from other raw foods (such as meat,
L10@P0: chicken, fish, eggs) to avoid cross contamination.
L11@P0: • Use a clean and sanitised chopping board and knife (a colour-coded
L12@P0: chopping board may also be appropriate).
L13@P0: • Wash fruit, vegetables and salad ingredients thoroughly in clean, drinkingquality water just before preparing and serving.
L14@P0: • Peel, trim or remove the skin or outer leaves (as appropriate).
L15@P0: • Remove any bruised or damaged parts.
L16@P0: Serving
L17@P0: • Use cut fruit and vegetables on the day they are prepared.
L18@P0: • Serve immediately after preparing or within 2 hours if possible – see the
L19@P0: ‘2-hour/4-hour’ rule below. If not serving within 2 hours, cover and store in
L20@P0: the fridge until serving (on the same day of preparation).
L21@P0: 2-hour/4-hour rule
L22@P0: The time potentially hazardous food can be safely held between 5°C and
L23@P0: 60°C is commonly referred to as the 2-hour/4-hour rule and is applied as follows:
L24@P0: Food businesses using the 2-hour/4-hour rule must demonstrate that the food is safe.
L25@P0: For more information visit foodauthority.nsw.gov.au
L26@P0: Fresh cut fruit and vegetables
L27@P0: 0 to 2 hours
L28@P0: Eat it, use it, or keep it at or
L29@P0: below 5°C, or at or above 60°C.
L30@P0: 2 to 4 hours
L31@P0: Eat it or use it.
L32@P0: More than 4 hours
L33@P0: Throw it away.
