L2@P0:  Cook chicken properly to kill bacteria and viruses that may cause foodborne illness.
L3@P0:  Prevent recontamination of cooked foods and contamination of other ready-to-eat foods.

L7@P0: Have a good work system for handling
L8@P0: ready-to-eat foods.

L10@P0: and meals.
L11@P0: Be extra careful when handling raw
L12@P0: chicken to avoid contamination of readyto-eat foods.
L13@P0:  Use different colour-coded knives and cutting boards for raw and

L15@P0:  Clean and sanitise the bench top after chicken preparation.
L16@P0:  Wash hands thoroughly after handling raw chicken.
L17@P0:  Store food covered – raw on the lower shelves and ready-to-eat food
L18@P0: on the upper shelves of the refrigerator or cool room.

L20@P0:  Display hot food at 60°C.

L61@P1: Temperature abuse
L62@P1: Most bacteria that cause foodborne
L63@P1: illness grow very quickly in warm
L64@P1: food. This can be prevented by
L65@P1: holding hot food at 60°C and cold
L66@P1: food at 5°C or less.
L67@P1: Cooking to the correct temperature
L68@P1: kills most food poisoning bacteria
L69@P1: and viruses. Some bacteria form a
L70@P1: spore (like a tough seed) that is not
L71@P1: killed by normal cooking processes.
L72@P1: Spores will activate in warm food
L73@P1: and can grow to very large numbers
L74@P1: if they have time. Correct storage
L75@P1: and prompt cooling and reheating of
L76@P1: food control this risk.
