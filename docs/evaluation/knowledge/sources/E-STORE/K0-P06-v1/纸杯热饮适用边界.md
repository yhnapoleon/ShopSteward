# 纸杯热饮适用边界
E-STORE | K0-P06-v1

## [section:1] 温度 / Temperature
E-SKU-CUP 纸杯只用于不超过80°C的饮品，不进入微波炉。Paper cups support drinks up to 80°C; no microwave use.

## [section:2] 密封 / Lid fit
只配 E-LID-90 的90 mm杯盖；压合后倒置检查是否渗漏。Use 90 mm E-LID-90 lids and perform a leak check.

## [section:3] 禁止替代 / No replacement
冷饮薄壁杯未通过热饮测试，不得替代本杯承装热饮。Untested thin-wall cold cups are not approved for hot service.
