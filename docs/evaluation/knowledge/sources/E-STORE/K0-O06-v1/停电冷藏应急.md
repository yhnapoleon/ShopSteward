# 停电冷藏应急
E-STORE | K0-O06-v1

## [section:1] 封存 / Seal
停电后保持冷柜关门，每15分钟记录温度；开始时间写在交接板。Keep the chiller closed and log temperature every 15 minutes.

## [section:2] 转移 / Transfer
温度超过6°C将酸奶隔离，由店长确认备用冷藏位置；不要自行认定安全。Above 6°C, quarantine yogurt and ask the manager to confirm backup storage.

## [section:3] 复电 / Restoration
复电不自动解除隔离，需复核连续温度记录。Power restoration alone does not clear quarantine.
