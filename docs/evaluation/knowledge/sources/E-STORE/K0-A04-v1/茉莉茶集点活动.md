# 茉莉茶集点活动
E-STORE | K0-A04-v1

## [section:1] 规则 / Rules
9月7日至9月14日，购买茉莉茶可获一枚纸质章；满5枚兑换茶包一袋。During September 7–14, each tea purchase earns one paper stamp; five redeem one tea bag.

## [section:2] 渠道 / Channel
仅店内柜台参加，外卖平台不参加，不与员工试饮叠加。In-store counter only; delivery platforms and staff tastings are excluded.

## [section:3] 稽核 / Audit
兑换时划销五枚章并登记日期，禁止拍照重复兑换。Cross out redeemed stamps and log the date; photos cannot be redeemed again.
